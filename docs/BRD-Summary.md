# Business Requirements Summary

*Condensed from the dashboard report; not a formally approved BRD.*

- **Business question:** Which machines or product/location combinations merit replenishment review?
- **Decision supported:** Prioritize stock and stale-unit investigation using volume, trend, and threshold views.
- **Scope:** Selected snack categories, vending machines, locations, and displayed time period.
- **Success measures:** Restock units and stale units; dollar waste and stockout outcomes are not reported.
- **Open item:** Verify that the **7,000** threshold is units and confirm the High/Low rule.
